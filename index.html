Trading Knowledge

Candlestick

Candlesticks show Open, High, Low and Close prices and help traders study price movement.

Support & Resistance

Support and resistance are important price areas where market reactions can occur.

FVG

Fair Value Gap is a price imbalance that traders may study as part of their market analysis.

OB

Order Block is a price area studied by some traders when analyzing institutional-style price movement.

RSI

RSI is a momentum indicator commonly used to study the strength of price movement.

EMA / SMA

Moving averages can help traders analyze trends and price direction.

VWAP

VWAP represents the volume-weighted average price and is commonly used for intraday analysis.

Trading Psychology

Discipline, patience and emotional control are important parts of learning trading.
